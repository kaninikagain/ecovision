About EcoVision — Intelligent Sustainability Hub

Our Mission

EcoVision bridges computer vision, real-time chemical contamination analysis, and municipal open data to empower everyday individuals to eliminate waste sorting confusion and prevent recycling stream contamination.

Gamification & Scoring System

Level progression follows cumulative XP curve: XP = 100 × n × (n − 1). You earn XP through verified scanning (+50 XP), first material bonuses (+30 XP), recycling confirmation (+25 XP), completed upcycling recipes (+40 XP), product wishlisting (+10 XP), voice assistance (+5 XP), and maintaining daily eco streaks (+20 XP).

Peer-Reviewed Data & Methodology

LCA emissions and energy factors are curated from EPA WARM v15, the Association of Plastic Recyclers (APR), The Aluminum Association, Project Drawdown, and OpenStreetMap Overpass APIs.

Privacy First Architecture

All camera streams, image classifications, and canvas grease analysis run 100% locally on your machine via client-side TensorFlow.js. No private photos or video frames are ever recorded or transmitted to third parties.
